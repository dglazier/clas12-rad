# CLAS12DetectorBuilder | Detector Dictionary

The `CLAS12DetectorBuilder` does not just blindly copy HIPO banks—it acts as a smart aggregator that resolves physical detector ambiguities and synthesizes high-level variables directly onto the combinatorial tracks[cite: 1]. 

Because HIPO relies on a relational database structure (e.g., the `REC::Calorimeter` bank contains a `pindex` column pointing to a track in `REC::Particle`), the framework maps, flattens, and projects these relationships into contiguous, single-particle arrays automatically[cite: 1].

Here is an exhaustive dictionary of all possible detector branches generated by the framework. Replace `NAME` with your defined particle name (e.g., `rec_proton_FD_Time`).

---

## Part 1: High-Level Region Abstractions (Synthesized Arrays)
These branches do **not** correspond to a single HIPO bank. Instead, they are dynamically generated by the builder using variadic SIMD vectorization to replicate the object-oriented fallback logic (e.g., choosing CTOF over CND)[cite: 1].

### Forward Detector (FD) Region
*   `rec_NAME_FD_DetEnergy`: **(Synthesized Sum)** Total valid energy deposited across *all* active layers of the Forward Electromagnetic Calorimeter (PCAL, ECIN, ECOUT)[cite: 1].
*   `rec_NAME_FD_DeltaEnergy`: **(Synthesized Fallback)** Energy loss (dE/dx) in the Forward Time-of-Flight (FTOF) scintillator. It searches in the order: FTOF1B -> FTOF1A -> FTOF2[cite: 1].
*   `rec_NAME_FD_Time`: **(Synthesized Evaluator)** Calculates the "best" time for a particle in the forward detector. It checks the particle's PID: if charged (or unknown), it uses the FTOF best time; if strictly neutral (Gamma, Neutron, Pi0), it falls back to the ECAL best time[cite: 1].
*   `rec_NAME_FD_Path`: **(Synthesized Evaluator)** The track's path length corresponding to `FD_Time`. It uses FTOF path for charged tracks and ECAL path for neutral tracks[cite: 1].
*   `rec_NAME_FD_Sector`: **(Synthesized Fallback)** Identifies the CLAS12 6-fold sector the particle hit, falling back in order from Drift Chamber (DC) -> FTOF1B -> PCAL[cite: 1].

### Central Detector (CD) Region
*   `rec_NAME_CD_DetEnergy`: **(Synthesized Sum)** The total valid energy deposited across all layers of the Central Neutron Detector (CND)[cite: 1].
*   `rec_NAME_CD_DeltaEnergy`: **(Direct Mapping)** Maps directly to the energy deposited in the Central Time-of-Flight (CTOF) scintillator[cite: 1].
*   `rec_NAME_CD_Time`: **(Synthesized Evaluator)** Calculates the "best" Central time. It prioritizes the high-resolution CTOF time, but if absent, falls back to the best available CND time[cite: 1].
*   `rec_NAME_CD_Path`: **(Synthesized Evaluator)** The path length corresponding to the selected `CD_Time` (CTOF falling back to CND)[cite: 1].

### Forward Tagger (FT) Region
*   `rec_NAME_FT_DetEnergy`: **(Direct Mapping)** The energy deposited in the Forward Tagger Calorimeter (FTCAL)[cite: 1].
*   `rec_NAME_FT_DeltaEnergy`: **(Direct Mapping)** The energy deposited in the Forward Tagger Hodoscope (FTHODO)[cite: 1].
*   `rec_NAME_FT_Time`: **(Direct Mapping)** The time response from the FTCAL[cite: 1].
*   `rec_NAME_FT_Path`: **(Synthesized Calculation)** Calculated geometrically on the fly using the FTCAL cluster coordinates: `sqrt(x^2 + y^2 + z^2)`[cite: 1].

### Backward Angle Neutron Detector (BAND) Region *(If configured)*
*   `rec_NAME_BAND_DetEnergy`: **(Synthesized Fallback)** Energy from the standard Time-of-Flight (TOF) layers (1-5) of the BAND detector[cite: 1].
*   `rec_NAME_BAND_DeltaEnergy`: **(Direct Mapping)** Energy from the BAND VETO layer (Layer 6)[cite: 1].
*   `rec_NAME_BAND_Time`: **(Synthesized Fallback)** Best time from the BAND TOF layers[cite: 1].
*   `rec_NAME_BAND_Path`: **(Synthesized Fallback)** Best path from the BAND TOF layers[cite: 1].

## Part 2: Low-Level Granular Detector Banks (Direct Bank Extraction)
These branches map directly to the `REC::*` HIPO banks. 

*Note on Layers (`_L#`):* Because of the dynamic geometry registry, segmented detectors will automatically append a layer suffix based on their configuration (e.g., PCAL is `_L1`, ECIN is `_L4`, ECOUT is `_L7`)[cite: 1]. Unsegmented detectors (like CTOF or HTCC) will not have a layer suffix.

### Calorimetry (`REC::Calorimeter` & `REC::CalExtras`)
*Extracted via `BuildCalorimeter(subdet, layer)`*[cite: 1]
*   `rec_NAME_ECAL_time_L#`: Time of the hit/cluster[cite: 1].
*   `rec_NAME_ECAL_energy_L#`: Energy deposited in this specific layer[cite: 1].
*   `rec_NAME_ECAL_path_L#`: Track path length from the interaction vertex to the calorimeter hit[cite: 1].
*   `rec_NAME_ECAL_chi2_L#`: Matching quality (chi2) of the track to the cluster[cite: 1].
*   `rec_NAME_ECAL_x_L#` / `_y_L#` / `_z_L#`: Hit position (X, Y, Z coordinates)[cite: 1].
*   `rec_NAME_ECAL_hx_L#` / `_hy_L#` / `_hz_L#`: Track intersection point at the detector face[cite: 1].
*   `rec_NAME_ECAL_lu_L#` / `_lv_L#` / `_lw_L#`: Distance on the U, V, and W strip coordinate systems[cite: 1].
*   `rec_NAME_ECAL_du_L#` / `_dv_L#` / `_dw_L#`: Cluster widths in the U, V, and W coordinates[cite: 1].
*   `rec_NAME_ECAL_m2u_L#` / `_m2v_L#` / `_m2w_L#`: Second moments (shape variables) of the cluster[cite: 1].
*   `rec_NAME_ECAL_m3u_L#` / `_m3v_L#` / `_m3w_L#`: Third moments of the cluster[cite: 1].
*   `rec_NAME_ECAL_status_L#`: Detector status word[cite: 1].
*   `rec_NAME_ECAL_sector_L#`: CLAS12 sector number (1-6)[cite: 1].
*   **Extras (`REC::CalExtras`)**: Advanced calibration values such as `_dbstU_L#`, `_rawEU_L#`, `_recEU_L#`, `_recDTU_L#`, and `_recFTU_L#`[cite: 1].

### Scintillators (`REC::Scintillator` & `REC::ScintExtras`)
*(FTOF, CND, CTOF, BAND)*
*Extracted via `BuildScintillator(subdet, layer)`*[cite: 1]
*   `rec_NAME_FTOF_time_L#`: Hit time in the scintillator paddle[cite: 1].
*   `rec_NAME_FTOF_energy_L#`: Energy deposited (dE/dx) in the paddle[cite: 1].
*   `rec_NAME_FTOF_path_L#`: Path length from vertex to scintillator hit[cite: 1].
*   `rec_NAME_FTOF_chi2_L#`: Track matching quality (chi2)[cite: 1].
*   `rec_NAME_FTOF_x_L#` / `_y_L#` / `_z_L#`: Global hit coordinates[cite: 1].
*   `rec_NAME_FTOF_hx_L#` / `_hy_L#` / `_hz_L#`: Track intersection projection[cite: 1].
*   `rec_NAME_FTOF_sector_L#`: Sector number[cite: 1].
*   `rec_NAME_FTOF_status_L#`: Hardware status word[cite: 1].
*   `rec_NAME_FTOF_component_L#`: Specific paddle/bar number hit within the sector[cite: 1].
*   **Extras (`REC::ScintExtras`)**: `_dedx_L#` (specific energy loss), `_size_L#` (cluster size), `_layermulti_L#` (multiplicity)[cite: 1].

### Track Core (`REC::Track`)
*(DC, CVT)*
*Extracted via `BuildTracker(subdet)`*[cite: 1]
*   `rec_NAME_DC_NDF`: Number of degrees of freedom in the track fit[cite: 1].
*   `rec_NAME_DC_sector`: Sector the track was found in[cite: 1].
*   `rec_NAME_DC_status`: Track fit status[cite: 1].
*   `rec_NAME_DC_q`: Track charge[cite: 1].
*   `rec_NAME_DC_chi2`: Fit chi2[cite: 1].

### Trajectories (`REC::Traj`)
*(DC, CVT)*
*Extracted via `BuildTrajectories(subdet, layer)`*[cite: 1]
*   `rec_NAME_DC_cx_L#` / `_cy_L#` / `_cz_L#`: Direction cosines of the track trajectory at this layer[cite: 1].
*   `rec_NAME_DC_x_L#` / `_y_L#` / `_z_L#`: Position coordinates of the trajectory[cite: 1].
*   `rec_NAME_DC_edge_L#`: Distance to the edge of the active detector volume (useful for fiducial cuts)[cite: 1].
*   `rec_NAME_DC_path_L#`: Integrated path length along the trajectory up to this specific layer[cite: 1].

### Cherenkov (`REC::Cherenkov`)
*(HTCC, LTCC, RICH)*
*Extracted via `BuildCherenkov(subdet)`*[cite: 1]
*   `rec_NAME_HTCC_nphe`: Number of photoelectrons detected (primary variable for PID)[cite: 1].
*   `rec_NAME_HTCC_time`: Cherenkov hit time[cite: 1].
*   `rec_NAME_HTCC_path`: Path length to the Cherenkov volume[cite: 1].
*   `rec_NAME_HTCC_sector`: Detector sector[cite: 1].
*   `rec_NAME_HTCC_chi2`: Track matching chi2[cite: 1].
*   `rec_NAME_HTCC_x` / `_y` / `_z`: Hit position[cite: 1].
*   `rec_NAME_HTCC_dtheta` / `_dphi`: Angle matching residuals[cite: 1].
*   `rec_NAME_HTCC_status`: Detector status word[cite: 1].

### Forward Tagger Components (`REC::ForwardTagger`)
*(FTCAL, FTHODO)*
*Extracted via `BuildForwardTagger(subdet, layer)`*[cite: 1]
*   `rec_NAME_FTCAL_time`, `_energy`, `_path`, `_status`, `_x`, `_y`, `_z`, `_chi2`: Standard hit kinematics[cite: 1].
*   `rec_NAME_FTCAL_dx` / `_dy`: Spatial cluster widths in x and y[cite: 1].
*   `rec_NAME_FTCAL_radius`: Radial distance of the cluster from the beamline[cite: 1].
*   `rec_NAME_FTCAL_size`: Number of crystals/elements comprising the cluster[cite: 1].

### Covariance Matrix (`REC::CovMat`)
*Extracted via `BuildCovMatrix()`*[cite: 1]
*   `rec_NAME_CovMat_C11` through `_C55`: Diagonal and off-diagonal elements of the 5x5 track fitting covariance matrix. These map directly to the raw `REC::CovMat` bank without layer dependencies[cite: 1].
